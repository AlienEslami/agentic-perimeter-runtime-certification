# Planned-time handover test, 26 September 2026

This extension is specified before delayed-handover outcomes are computed. It
keeps every completed synchronous benchmark record intact.

Select the eight development extremes formed by widths 1/50 vehicles,
residuals 60/300 vehicles/hour, deadlines 2750/2950 vehicles and Region-2
weight 4; add primary contract D027 and all eight held-out contracts. This
gives 17 contracts. Test planned handover delays of 60, 120 and 300 seconds
from minute 20, for 51 cases.

At minute 20 an already certified incumbent is active through the absolute
minute-60 expiry. Use the original continuation when its same-contract proof
passes, otherwise the previously computed, certified fresh reserve. The
initial certification is a prerequisite, outside the replacement latency.

The incumbent executes during all replacement computation. Verify its prefix
from the original information box to the planned handover time. Convert its
endpoint metric box to a physical outer box using exact binary-rational matrix
inversion and outward-rounded supports. No new measurement or narrowing of the
original uncertainty is supplied. Re-optimize from this future center through
the unchanged minute-60 deadline, then independently certify from the whole
propagated box. A numerical optimizer may use the future center as a forecast;
only the checker grants execution authority for the full future set.

Align proposal and incumbent on the union of their absolute command-change
times before testing repair weights 0, 0.05, ..., 1. Check every preceding
weight. A fresh endpoint certificate is required for this enlarged activation
box before invoking the finite-grid endpoint guarantee. When that endpoint
proof does not close, the original incumbent retains its original correlated
proof and absolute schedule.

The complete prefix propagation, optimization, endpoint check, original-plan
check and repair search are timed on the actual processor. Install a passing
replacement only if all required computation finished before its planned
handover slot; otherwise retain the original verified plan. A nonpreemptive
check may finish after its budget; its late verdict grants no replacement
authority. Timing records are host-specific. This implements a scheduled
handover deadline and preserves the original absolute service deadline.

Evaluate each executed policy at all 128 original initial-box corners and
the five existing residual profiles: 640 traffic trajectories per case. Simulate
the incumbent before handover and the accepted replacement thereafter. Check
the propagated information-box membership at handover and every sampled
physical and final service row. Independently replay the common adverse
trajectory with DOP853, splitting absolute gate and disturbance changes.

The finite-plan theorem applies through minute 60. A slot-aware planning
implementation can accept a future-dated certificate; it must preserve the
incumbent's proof before that activation time. This experiment has no terminal
backup construction beyond the recorded expiry.

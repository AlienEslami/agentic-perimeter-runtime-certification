MANUSCRIPT CONSISTENCY AND NOTATION REVIEW
16 October 2026

Current versions: reduced 45 pages; complete 79 pages, non-IEEE layouts.
This round retains the requested notation and consistency corrections.
The manuscript-preparation AI declaration is omitted from BOTH versions,
as explicitly clarified by the author after the initial review.

ASSESSMENT

I agree with the corresponding-author, method-name, repair-column and
appendix-equation inconsistencies. Several notation overlaps were real;
others were already distinguished by font, subscript or local scope.
The changes below prioritize symbols that readers repeatedly encounter.

Both versions name Jiangbo Yu as corresponding author. Names, author order,
email addresses and contributions otherwise retain the approved wording.

Both versions use Base interval, Positivity/queue and Signed; Nominal-plan
repair; and Repairs executed. Chronological uses of original, such as an
original expiry or previously certified continuation, retain that meaning.
Five shared results-table captions now use consistent method definitions,
units, replay denominators, pass/fail meanings and executed-repair counts.
The complete paper's Figure H.2 legend now says All three enclosures and
Signed. Its caption identifies the common target-1600 execution and the
separate target-1625 initial design. Saved arrays, curves and axes are
unchanged. A portable plotting script and frozen data accompany the source.

UNITS

Physical hourly flows use veh/h; accumulated traffic time uses veh-h,
defined as vehicle-hours in the notation table. Inventory remains measured
in 100-vehicle units where specified. Different dimensions are intentional:
veh/h is a flow rate; veh-h measures time accumulated across vehicles.

The reported MFD peak 6.303 veh/s is expressed as 22,691 veh/h (rounded).
The reference Task-R demand 1.6 veh/s per class is expressed as 5760 veh/h,
equivalently 57.6 normalized inventory units/h. The reference CLF controller
retains its native veh/s scaling, now explicitly obtained by multiplying
the normalized hourly field and input columns by 100/3600. Its nonlinear
law would change if its rate scale were changed without this conversion.
Seconds used for integration steps and computation times remain appropriate.

NOTATION CHANGES IN BOTH PAPERS, INCLUDING THE COMPLETE APPENDICES

  Total inventory M is retained; the Metzler comparison matrix is overline M.
  Repair weight vartheta becomes lambda_rep; grid Theta becomes G_rep;
    its number of intervals is N_rep and the search takes at most N_rep+1
    checks. Task record theta_i is retained. G_rep avoids reusing the
    exterior-arrival symbol Lambda.
  The contract remains calligraphic C, already distinct from ordinary C.
    The checker is Cert_t; the request compiler is Comp. UpdateTask names
    the parsed task transition, and SelectTask returns the active objective
    and remaining duration. These functions have different signatures.
  Scalar and network admission capacities C and C_i become kappa and kappa_i.
  Delay change C_r becomes Delta D_omega, and priority improvement I_r
    becomes Delta J_omega. Both are percentage changes; their signs and
    denominators are unchanged. Recovered benefit is B_omega. An obsolete
    J_2/varrho entry was replaced by the actually defined B_omega.
  Computational C_d,C_q,C_J become cost_def,cost_queue,cost_Jac.
    The transformed constraint row C_i is written directly as a_x^T L^{-1}.
  The matrix-exponential integral Q becomes Psi. Task Q is retained.
  Primitive internal demands use d^int, including destination components
    and the demand vector; sensing widths retain d. Plan distance is dist.
  Quadratic coefficients a,b,c become alpha_2,alpha_1,alpha_0.
  The admission-branch index becomes varpi. Command block b remains b.
    The computational branch count becomes n_br. Varpi avoids colliding
    with the existing scalar comparison function chi(a,s).
  The matrix-tail scalar z=alpha*h becomes sigma=alpha*h; queue z remains z.
  Terminal uncertainty eta in calligraphic E becomes w_B in W_B, avoiding
    service threshold eta and distinguishing general backup uncertainty
    from the finite-plan arrival residual w.
  Task expiry e_i becomes tau_i^exp; coordinate vector e_i is retained.
  Simulation realization r becomes omega; queue count r becomes n_q.
    Recentring error omega becomes delta^rec to free omega for realizations.
    Service-row index r and radius r_l retain their established meanings.
  Absolute activation time s becomes t_a. Local integration time s remains
    s. The decision record is written directly as calligraphic S_{t_i},
    avoiding the unnecessary plain-letter alias s_i.
  Requirements T_i and information I_i were already calligraphic T_i and
    I_i. Their distinct typography is retained rather than renamed again.

in each version. All theorem/proposition/corollary statements and proofs

no oversized floats. All 124 pages were rendered and reviewed; affected
mathematical, table, figure and declaration pages were checked in detail.
Independent source reviews checked the symbol substitutions and native CLF

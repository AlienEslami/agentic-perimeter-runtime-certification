PLAN EXPIRY

The finite-plan limitation remains in both manuscripts. Certification of
one plan establishes feasibility through its recorded expiry E. Rejection
preserves that expiry. Coverage afterward requires either a replacement
installed by E with a later certified expiry, or a terminal backup
certified for the state reached at E and activated at that time.
Without either continuation, the finite-plan result gives no guarantee
after E. Traffic continues to evolve physically after a certificate expires.

Theorem 4 addresses indefinite continuation under its additional terminal
conditions. It requires a certified invariant set and backup policy,
terminal-set inclusion, implementable transitions, service coverage,
the stated uncertainty/execution assumptions and locally finite
installations. These conditions remain in the statement and proof.

expiries. The longer manuscript also retains the historical outage account
stating explicitly that those simulations end at expiry. The revision adds
a clear scope paragraph before the terminal-backup discussion in each
main text. The discussion now specifically identifies the conditions for
indefinite continuation through the backup.
